## 💼 Jobs

| Name                           | Full Prompt                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Job Description Gap Analysis   | Compare my resume with the job description. Identify the most important required skills and keywords, skills I already have but am underselling, true experience gaps, and wording gaps that can be fixed without inventing experience. |
| ATS Keyword Extractor          | Identify and rank the 15 most important ATS keywords from the job description. Group them into Hard Skills, Tools/Software, and Soft Skills, then show where they can naturally fit into my resume.                                     |
| ATS Resume Scan                | Review my resume for ATS compatibility. Flag missing keywords, unclear headings, formatting issues, and anything that may be difficult for an ATS to parse.                                                                             |
| Targeted Resume Tailor         | Tailor my resume to the job description by reordering, emphasizing, and rewriting only my existing experience. Prioritize the most relevant qualifications without inventing anything.                                                  |
| Achievement Bullet Rewrite     | Rewrite my resume bullets using: Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z. Start with strong action verbs, avoid repetition, remove filler, and keep each bullet concise.                                                              |
| Quantified Achievement Builder | Strengthen each bullet with measurable impact. If a metric is missing, use `[ADD METRIC]` or `[PLACEHOLDER]` and suggest what data I could check to verify it.                                                                          |
| Bullet Tightener               | Shorten my resume bullets while preserving the strongest action, accomplishment, and measurable result. Keep each bullet to about two lines or less.                                                                                    |
| Experience Section Rewrite     | Rewrite my work experience with stronger action verbs, clearer language, relevant keywords, and measurable accomplishments while preserving all facts.                                                                                  |
| Transferable Skills Bridge     | Compare my previous experience with my target field. Identify what to emphasize, de-emphasize, and reword so my transferable skills are clear.                                                                                          |
| Promotion Highlighter          | Rewrite multiple positions at the same company so promotions, increased responsibility, expanded scope, and career progression are immediately clear.                                                                                   |
| Skills Section Builder         | Create an ATS-friendly skills section for my target role. Group skills into relevant categories, rank them by importance, and remove low-value or generic skills.                                                                       |
| Recruiter Resume Critique      | Evaluate my resume for relevance, clarity, credibility, impact, and competitiveness. Identify the highest-priority changes before rewriting anything.                                                                                   |
| Customer Success Resume Tailor | Tailor my resume for customer success or account management roles by emphasizing retention, onboarding, adoption, expansion, account growth, satisfaction, and churn reduction.                                                         |
| LinkedIn Profile Alignment     | Review my LinkedIn headline, About section, skills, and experience together. Flag inconsistencies and recommend changes so the entire profile communicates one clear professional identity.                                             |
| LinkedIn Experience Optimizer  | Rewrite my LinkedIn experience using relevant recruiter keywords while keeping the wording natural, readable, and accurate.                                                                                                             |
| LinkedIn Skills Builder        | Generate the most valuable LinkedIn skills for my target role and identify the 3–5 most important ones to feature prominently.                                                                                                          |

<a id="slash-commands"></a>

## ⌨️ Slash Commands

- **`/human`** — Rewrites robotic text to sound natural and conversational.
- **`/shorten` or `/dltr`** — Condenses the output into a brief, direct answer.
- **`/formal`** — Switches the tone to professional.
- **`/listify`** — Formats the response as a clear bulleted list.
