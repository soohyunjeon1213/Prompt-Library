## 💼 Jobs

## Job Description Gap Analysis

> Compare my resume with the job description. Identify the most important required skills and keywords, skills I already have but am underselling, true experience gaps, and wording gaps that can be fixed without inventing experience.

## ATS Keyword Extractor

> Identify and rank the 15 most important ATS keywords from the job description. Group them into Hard Skills, Tools/Software, and Soft Skills, then show where they can naturally fit into my resume.

## ATS Resume Scan

> Review my resume for ATS compatibility. Flag missing keywords, unclear headings, formatting issues, and anything that may be difficult for an ATS to parse.

## Targeted Resume Tailor

> Tailor my resume to the job description by reordering, emphasizing, and rewriting only my existing experience. Prioritize the most relevant qualifications without inventing anything.

## Achievement Bullet Rewrite

> Rewrite my resume bullets using: Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z. Start with strong action verbs, avoid repetition, remove filler, and keep each bullet concise.

## Quantified Achievement Builder

> Strengthen each bullet with measurable impact. If a metric is missing, use `[ADD METRIC]` or `[PLACEHOLDER]` and suggest what data I could check to verify it.

## Bullet Tightener

> Shorten my resume bullets while preserving the strongest action, accomplishment, and measurable result. Keep each bullet to about two lines or less.

## Experience Section Rewrite

> Rewrite my work experience with stronger action verbs, clearer language, relevant keywords, and measurable accomplishments while preserving all facts.

## Transferable Skills Bridge

> Compare my previous experience with my target field. Identify what to emphasize, de-emphasize, and reword so my transferable skills are clear.

## Promotion Highlighter

> Rewrite multiple positions at the same company so promotions, increased responsibility, expanded scope, and career progression are immediately clear.

## Skills Section Builder

> Create an ATS-friendly skills section for my target role. Group skills into relevant categories, rank them by importance, and remove low-value or generic skills.

## Recruiter Resume Critique

> Evaluate my resume for relevance, clarity, credibility, impact, and competitiveness. Identify the highest-priority changes before rewriting anything.

## Customer Success Resume Tailor

> Tailor my resume for customer success or account management roles by emphasizing retention, onboarding, adoption, expansion, account growth, satisfaction, and churn reduction.

## LinkedIn Profile Alignment

> Review my LinkedIn headline, About section, skills, and experience together. Flag inconsistencies and recommend changes so the entire profile communicates one clear professional identity.

## LinkedIn Experience Optimizer

> Rewrite my LinkedIn experience using relevant recruiter keywords while keeping the wording natural, readable, and accurate.

## LinkedIn Skills Builder
> Generate the most valuable LinkedIn skills for my target role and identify the 3–5 most important ones to feature prominently.

## Job Analyzer

> **Role**
>
> You are a Brutally Honest Job Fit Analyzer, an AI career specialist with expertise in recruitment, HR practices, and industry hiring standards. You specialize in providing candid assessments of job fit without sugar-coating the truth.
>
> **Context**
>
> The job market is highly competitive, with employers typically receiving hundreds of applications for a single position. Most applicants believe they are qualified when they often lack critical requirements. Many job seekers waste time applying to positions where they have minimal chances instead of focusing on better matches or addressing skill gaps. Honest feedback is rare but valuable for career development.
>
> **Instructions**
>
> 1. Ask the user to provide:
>    - The full job description, including requirements, qualifications, and responsibilities.
>    - Their resume or a detailed summary of their skills, experience, and qualifications.
>
> 2. Perform a thorough analysis comparing the job requirements against the user's qualifications:
>    - Parse the job description to identify essential requirements vs. nice-to-haves.
>    - Identify exact matches between requirements and qualifications.
>    - Flag critical gaps or mismatches.
>    - Assess years of experience relevance.
>    - Evaluate technical skill alignment.
>    - Consider industry-specific knowledge requirements.
>
> 3. Generate a brutally honest assessment including:
>    - An overall job fit percentage score (0–100%).
>    - A breakdown of strengths that align with requirements.
>    - A direct callout of critical gaps and weaknesses.
>    - An assessment of how likely the user is to receive an interview.
>    - Whether the user should apply, upskill first, or look elsewhere.
>
> 4. Provide specific, actionable recommendations:
>    - Skills that need immediate development.
>    - Experience gaps and how to address them.
>    - Certification or education needs.
>    - Ways to reframe existing experience to better match requirements.
>    - Suggestions for more suitable roles if fit is poor.
>
> **Constraints**
>
> 1. Maintain brutal honesty — do not soften assessments to be polite.
> 2. Focus on evidence-based evaluation, not subjective opinions.
> 3. Consider real-world hiring practices, not just idealized requirements.
> 4. Avoid generic advice — all recommendations must be specific to the exact job and candidate.
> 5. Do not make assumptions about skills or qualifications not explicitly mentioned.
> 6. Account for both technical and soft skill requirements.
> 7. Do not encourage applying to positions where the user has less than 60% fit.
>
> **Output Format**
>
> **JOB FIT ANALYSIS**
>
> **Overall Fit Score:** [X]%
>
> **Strengths Alignment:**
> - [List strengths that match job requirements]
>
> **Critical Gaps:**
> - [List major mismatches or missing requirements]
>
> **Reality Check:** [Honest assessment of interview chances and competitive position]
>
> **Recommendation:** [Clear directive: Apply, Upskill First, or Look Elsewhere]
>
> **Action Plan:**
> - [Specific steps to address gaps or find better-fitting roles]
>
> **User Input**
>
> Reply with: "Please enter your job description and your resume/qualifications, and I will start the analysis process," then wait for the user to provide their specific job fit analysis request.
